# 🎯 Pinboard Panic

Welcome to **Pinboard Panic**, a ridiculous little darts game where your aim is questionable and the target deserves it.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open the game: **https://rainykermit.github.io/pinboard/**
2. Watch the horizontal and vertical aim bars move continuously.
3. Click each bar to lock that direction. The first click locks left-to-right aim; the second locks up-and-down aim.
4. Click **Throw Dart** (or press **Space**) to launch your dart and watch it fly into the pinboard.
5. Score points based on how close you get to the center of the target.

You get **five darts** per round. Try to beat your score and earn the title of Office Legend!

## 🏆 Scoring

- **100 points** — Direct hit!
- **60 points** — Close enough.
- **25 points** — Technically nearby.
- **5 points** — A creative miss.

## 🔄 Play Again

After all five darts, your final score and title appear. Click **Play Again** to start another round.

## 💻 Play Anywhere

The game runs directly in your web browser on desktop and mobile. No downloads, accounts, or installations are required.

**Aim carefully. Miss spectacularly. Blame the pinboard.**
